What the app is

Melted Talker is a small phone app for reading something in another language. You paste a message, a sign, a paragraph, or a few words. It names the language it found, then writes the English on the same screen. You can copy that English, or clear the page and try another line.

You can also send text in from somewhere else. In another app, choose Share and pick Melted Talker. The words land in the box and the translation starts. With the side tab on, long-press text in any app, tap Translate on the edge, and the translation comes up in that one tap.

The samples on the first screen are Spanish, French, Russian, Bulgarian, Japanese, Korean, Mandarin, Turkish, and Arabic. Those are only examples. A line in another language is fine too. A very short line can be hard to place, and the app says so when it is unsure. If the text is already English, it says that instead of pretending it translated something.

The words are sent to Google Translate so the language can be detected and the English written. The phone needs an internet connection for that. A grammar brain stays on the phone and keeps learning from grammar in the text. Keep a passage under 12,000 characters.

Read me

You need an Android phone. The app is the file To-English.apk, version 1.0. It is not in the Play Store.

  1. Download the APK at the bottom of this page.
  2. Open the file. If the phone stops you, allow this browser or the Files app to install unknown apps, then open it again.
  3. Open Melted Talker, paste or type, and tap Translate. Or share text into it from another app.

The English can be copied from the app. After you open it once, it stays on in the background, including after the phone restarts. Allow the notification and the battery prompt so the phone does not shut it off. The page starts empty each time you open it.
