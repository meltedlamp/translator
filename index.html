Voice typing

Talk, then let go

Hold Right Ctrl, speak the way you think, and release. The words are typed where your cursor already is. “Um” is left out, and “4, actually 6” keeps 6. Tap Right Ctrl twice, or press Ctrl + Alt + Space, when you want both hands free. Right-click the tray icon and choose Type speech in English when you want another language written in English.

On the computer, hold the right Ctrl key and speak. The words are typed into whatever you were writing.
